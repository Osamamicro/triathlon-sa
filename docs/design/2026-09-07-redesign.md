# Public site redesign — the Federation's own identity

Written 2026-09-07, before implementation. Supersedes the "Night Race" navy/teal exploration that
the Razor port inherited from the static prototype.

## 1. The idea

**The mark is the course.** The Federation's logo is three curved blades chasing each other around a
circle — swim, bike and run drawn as one continuous loop. That is already the best idea the brand
has, and the old skin threw it away in favour of a generic navy tech look with a rainbow gradient
sprayed over every rule, heading and border.

So: the site is built out of the mark. The Federation green becomes the ground (a deep green-black
dark theme, a green-cast white light theme) and the *only* general-purpose accent. The three
discipline colours stop being decoration and become a strict code that appears **only where a
discipline is actually named** — the leg strip, distance pills, the season timeline's calendar dots,
the map markers, the footer's three-segment strip. Everywhere the old design reached for the
rainbow — nav underline, eyebrow dashes, stat-tile stripes, banner rules, headline gradient — it now
reaches for one green.

The repeating structural device is the **lane rule**: a hairline between sections carrying a short
solid-green segment at the inline start, like the lane marker painted at the head of a pool lane.
One device, brand-owned, RTL-native, and it replaces four different kinds of decorative rule.

## 4. Type

Rajdhani goes. It is a condensed esports face that has nothing to do with the Federation, and it
forced English into uppercase while Arabic stayed sentence case — two languages, two personalities.

**Display is now Tajawal in both scripts** (700/800), which is the family closest to the geometric,
flat-terminal Arabic of the official wordmark, and which the site already self-hosts for Arabic.
Body stays IBM Plex Sans / IBM Plex Sans Arabic; telemetry stays IBM Plex Mono. That is a real
contrast axis (geometric display against a humanist grotesque body) and it deletes three font files.

Headings lose `text-transform:uppercase` and the positive tracking; they run sentence case at
`-0.015em` with a 3.4× scale contrast against body. English and Arabic now share one set of rules,
with Arabic taking +6 % size and +0.12 line-height, so `html[lang=ar]` overrides drop from 21 to 6.

## 5. Motion

Unchanged in budget, tightened in intent. Everything stays behind `prefers-reduced-motion`.

- Hero course draws once on load (it tells you the race is swim → T1 → bike → T2 → run).
- Counters count, statistics bars fill, both on first intersection.
- Scroll reveal shortened to 14 px / 480 ms so it stops reading as a uniform template.
- The map's marker halos no longer pulse forever; they pulse on hover, focus and selection, which
  is feedback rather than ambient noise.

## 6. Per-page changes

- **Home** — hero gets a flat green band, the mark bleeding off the inline end, and breathing room
  where the right half used to be empty; the gradient headline becomes solid green emphasis; the
  stat band becomes a timing board (hairline-divided cells, no card, no side stripe) so it stops
  being the third identical card grid on the page.
- **Events / event** — event card leads with a bib-style date block; the calendar chip is green,
  the status chip stays semantic; gallery placeholders become quiet ruled frames instead of pastel
  washes.
- **Season timeline** — the rail is one green line; the type colour lives in the dots and the map
  markers, where it means "which calendar". The map gets more width and a stronger land fill.
- **Governance / documents** — the nine identical rounded cards become a filing list: hairline
  rows, category and year in mono columns, download affordance on hover and focus.
- **Statistics** — each chart is one series, so each chart is one colour; cycling the discipline
  hues down six regions made the colour carry no information. Tiles follow the timing board, and
  years stop rendering with a thousands separator ("2,023").
- **Join / training / rules / news / contact / 404 / 500** — inherit the tokens, the quieter
  eyebrow, the lane rule and the new heading scale.
- **Header** — the official mark replaces the invented triangle; nav is sentence case in both
  languages; the 390 px overflow that pushed the burger 10 px off-screen is fixed.
- **Footer** — the rainbow bar becomes three discrete swim/bike/run segments, which is the code
  rather than a gradient.

## 6b. Defects found and fixed while redesigning

Three bugs that pre-date this pass and would have failed a QA round anyway:

1. **The statistics charts rendered no data.** `.bar-fill` is a `<span>` inside a grid item, so it
   was never blockified; `width` and `height` were dropped and every bar showed as an empty track.
   Fixed with `display:block` on the fill.
2. **The mobile menu bled the page through** (the P2 carried over from the Week 1 QA report). The
   drawer is a descendant of the header's `backdrop-filter` layer, where a `rgba(...,.99)`
   background composites over the blurred backdrop instead of covering it. Fixed by making
   `--nav-bg` fully opaque.
3. **The header overflowed 390 px viewports by 10 px**, pushing the burger off-screen. Fixed by
   tightening the header below 560 px and dropping the brand sub-line.

Not fixed, deliberately: `.bar-fill` animates `width` rather than a transform. It is one short,
one-shot transition on ten small elements, the element it animates is the datum itself, and
switching to `scaleX` would need `transform-origin` juggling in RTL for no measurable gain.

# Polish round 1 — answering the design critique

Continuation of the pass above, not a new direction. Judged against
`docs/qa/2026-09-07-design-critique-redesign.md`: every P0 and every P1, the five ranked moves,
and the P2s that were under ten minutes. The do-not-change list survives intact — the type system,
the documents library as a filing list, and the bib date block and leg strip are all untouched
except where the critique named the fix.

Screenshots are in the session scratchpad under `polish/`; the critique's own set under `critic/`
is the "before". Both are named per cell below.

## What changed, by finding

### P0

**P0-1, sticky map collides with the timeline at 390.** Root cause was ordering, not a missing
query: `@media (max-width:920px){.map-wrap{position:static}}` already existed but sat *above* the
`.map-wrap{position:sticky}` rule it was meant to override, so at equal specificity the sticky
rule won. The override moved below the base rule and gained `margin-block-end`
(`wwwroot/css/site.css`, `.map-wrap`). The map is now the season's overview once, then scrolls
away. Before `critic/ar-timeline-390-sticky-overlap.png` → after `polish/ar-timeline-390-dark.png`,
`polish/ar-timeline-390-scrolled.png`, `polish/en-timeline-390-dark.png`.

**P0-2, every headline statistic shipped as `0`.** `_StatBand.cshtml` prints `Number(k.Value)`
(and the suffix) as the tile's first text node and keeps `data-count`, `data-suffix` and
`<span class="plus">`. `Statistics.cshtml` writes `style="width:N%"` beside `data-w` on every
`.bar-fill`. In `site.js`, `animateCounter` returns immediately under `prefers-reduced-motion`
and otherwise writes `0` itself before tweening; the bars collapse to zero inside the observer's
first callback for each bar, so if the observer never reports, the served width stands. Verified
at the wire: `curl /ar | grep stat-value` returns `data-count="1284">١٬٢٨٤`. Before
`critic/en-home-1440-dark-FULL.png` → after `polish/en-home-1440-stats.png`,
`polish/ar-home-1440-stats.png`, `polish/en-stats-1440-dark.png`, `polish/ar-stats-1440-dark.png`.

**P0-3, the hero course was not mirrored in RTL.** The three strokes and the two dots are wrapped
in `<g class="course-legs">` and the T1/T2 labels in `<g class="course-marks">`
(`Areas/Public/Pages/Index.cshtml`). Under `html[dir="rtl"]` both groups take
`transform:scaleX(-1)` about the viewBox centre and each `<text>` counter-flips with
`transform-box:fill-box`. The swim wave now sits above the swim cell of the strip, the finish dot
at the inline end, and T1 precedes T2 in reading order. The `stroke-dashoffset` draw is untouched
and still runs from the start of the race. Before `critic/ar-home-1440-hero.png` → after
`polish/ar-home-1440-dark.png`, `polish/ar-home-390-dark.png`, `polish/ar-home-1440-light.png`.

### P1

**P1-4, the discipline code was decoration on four surfaces.** Four edits, one rule: amber only
ever means run.

- The stat band lost `--stat-c` and the per-cell `.stat::before` stripe entirely; it is a framed
  hairline grid and nothing else (`_StatBand.cshtml`, `site.css`). `SeedStats.cs` no longer
  assigns a colour to a KPI at all, so the cycle cannot come back through the dashboard.
- The PDF badge is one `--accent`; `DocRowModel` lost its `Color` and `CategoryColor`.
- The contact cards, the join page's four steps and its pathway nodes lost their seeded colours
  (`SeedPages.cs`). The rules cards and the training cards keep theirs, because those name a
  discipline.
- The calendar chips (`chip-competition` / `chip-community`) are one outline chip in the ink; the
  word does the work. Where there is no room for a word — the map markers and the timeline rail —
  the two calendars are told apart by fill instead: solid for the national series, hollow for
  community, both in the Federation green. The legend follows (`i-solid` / `i-hollow` / `i-sel`).

Before `critic/en-home-1440-stats.png`, `critic/en-stats-1440-dark.png`,

**P1-5, two numeral systems in one card.** New `PublicText.Distance(string?)`: in Arabic it maps
`m`/`km` after a number to `م`/`كم`, converts the digits and the decimal point (`٢٫٥كم`), and
leaves English alone. Called from `_Distances.cshtml` and the event page's leg strip; the home
tri-strip's three hand-written Arabic strings were rewritten in Arabic-Indic digits. The system
chosen is Arabic-Indic throughout Arabic, matching the dates, times and KPI values already there.
The critique recommended the opposite (Latin everywhere); the ruling to standardise on
Arabic-Indic came with the task and is recorded as an open question in the task report. After
`polish/ar-timeline-390-scrolled.png`, `polish/ar-events-1440-light.png`,
`polish/ar-home-1440-dark.png`.

**P1-6, `--line` failed WCAG 1.4.11.** Split into three weights rather than raising one token to a
heavy 3:1 hairline on every card edge:

- `--line` is decorative (row, card and section hairlines) and was raised for legibility only —
  measured on `--surface`, 1.54 → **1.99** dark and 1.37 → **1.63** light.
- `--line-strong` structures a block: 2.44 dark, 2.06 light.
- `--edge` is new and carries every user-interface component — inputs, selects, segmented
  controls, the ghost button, the header pills, the download affordance — at **3.13:1** dark and
  **3.12:1** light, which is what 1.4.11 actually asks for.

Because a light card is separated from the page by an 1.11:1 tone step, the light theme also gets
a tinted shadow on `.card`, `.stat-band`, `.map-wrap`, `.table-wrap`, `.tri-strip`, `.step` and
`.cta-banner` — a non-colour boundary where a boundary is needed. Ratios measured live with
`playwright-cli eval`, alpha composited over the real backdrop. Before
`critic/en-rules-1440-light.png`, `critic/en-event-1440-light.png` → after
`polish/en-rules-1440-light.png`, `polish/en-event-1440-light.png`,
`polish/ar-register-1440-light.png`.

**P1-7, the registration dropdowns looked like text inputs.** `select.input` now carries the same
inline-SVG chevron the documents filter uses, positioned with `background-position` and mirrored
under `[dir=rtl]`; checkboxes and radios are 24 px (SC 2.5.8, measured 24×24); the four required
labels carry an `aria-hidden` asterisk explained by a note above the form; the date field gained a
`يوم / شهر / سنة` hint, since it is the one left-to-right control in a mirrored form. After
`polish/ar-register-1440-light.png`, `polish/en-register-1440-dark.png`,
`polish/ar-register-390-dark.png`.

**P1-8, 6 px map markers.** Each marker gained a transparent `circle.hit` at r=26 user units —
measured 25×25 px at 1440. 26 is the largest radius that keeps Jeddah and Taif, 56 units apart,
from overlapping. At 390 the whole map is 350 px wide and the same circle is 18 px; the equivalent
control there is the timeline card itself, which is full-bleed and reaches the same city.

**P1-9, event card titles did not align across a row.** `.event-card .date-block` is a column

**P1-10, empty dashed gallery frames.** The gallery block only renders when the event has images

**P1-11, six clubs, one identical sentence.** The sentence is gone, and rather than leave six

(`_Blocks/_Clubs.cshtml`, `.club-list` in `site.css`). Before `critic/en-join-1440-dark.png` →

**P1-12, the map and the timeline never connected.** `timeline.js` observes every `.tl-item`
through a `rootMargin:"-45% 0px -45% 0px"` band, so exactly one item is current, and lights that

`aria-live` node whose sentence is server-rendered per culture (`{city}, events: {n}` /

**P1-13, the statistics page opened by talking about the CMS.** The intro is now "Who races, where

**P1-14, the em-dash as default connector.** Swept out of every user-visible string in
