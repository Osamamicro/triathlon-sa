# Public site redesign — the Federation's own identity

Written 2026-09-07, before implementation. Supersedes the "Night Race" navy/teal exploration that
the Razor port inherited from the static prototype.

## 1. The idea

**The mark is the course.** The Federation's logo is three curved blades chasing each other around a
circle — swim, bike and run drawn as one continuous loop. That is already the best idea the brand
has, and the old skin threw it away in favour of a generic navy tech look with a rainbow gradient
sprayed over every rule, heading and border.

So: the site is built out of the mark. The Federation green becomes the ground (a deep green-black
dark theme, a green-cast white light theme) and the *only* general-purpose accent. The three
discipline colours stop being decoration and become a strict code that appears **only where a
discipline is actually named** — the leg strip, distance pills, the season timeline's calendar dots,
the map markers, the footer's three-segment strip. Everywhere the old design reached for the
rainbow — nav underline, eyebrow dashes, stat-tile stripes, banner rules, headline gradient — it now
reaches for one green.

The repeating structural device is the **lane rule**: a hairline between sections carrying a short
solid-green segment at the inline start, like the lane marker painted at the head of a pool lane.
One device, brand-owned, RTL-native, and it replaces four different kinds of decorative rule.

## 4. Type

Rajdhani goes. It is a condensed esports face that has nothing to do with the Federation, and it
forced English into uppercase while Arabic stayed sentence case — two languages, two personalities.

**Display is now Tajawal in both scripts** (700/800), which is the family closest to the geometric,
flat-terminal Arabic of the official wordmark, and which the site already self-hosts for Arabic.
Body stays IBM Plex Sans / IBM Plex Sans Arabic; telemetry stays IBM Plex Mono. That is a real
contrast axis (geometric display against a humanist grotesque body) and it deletes three font files.

Headings lose `text-transform:uppercase` and the positive tracking; they run sentence case at
`-0.015em` with a 3.4× scale contrast against body. English and Arabic now share one set of rules,
with Arabic taking +6 % size and +0.12 line-height, so `html[lang=ar]` overrides drop from 21 to 6.

## 5. Motion

Unchanged in budget, tightened in intent. Everything stays behind `prefers-reduced-motion`.

- Hero course draws once on load (it tells you the race is swim → T1 → bike → T2 → run).
- Counters count, statistics bars fill, both on first intersection.
- Scroll reveal shortened to 14 px / 480 ms so it stops reading as a uniform template.
- The map's marker halos no longer pulse forever; they pulse on hover, focus and selection, which
  is feedback rather than ambient noise.

## 6. Per-page changes

- **Home** — hero gets a flat green band, the mark bleeding off the inline end, and breathing room
  where the right half used to be empty; the gradient headline becomes solid green emphasis; the
  stat band becomes a timing board (hairline-divided cells, no card, no side stripe) so it stops
  being the third identical card grid on the page.
- **Events / event** — event card leads with a bib-style date block; the calendar chip is green,
  the status chip stays semantic; gallery placeholders become quiet ruled frames instead of pastel
  washes.
- **Season timeline** — the rail is one green line; the type colour lives in the dots and the map
  markers, where it means "which calendar". The map gets more width and a stronger land fill.
- **Governance / documents** — the nine identical rounded cards become a filing list: hairline
  rows, category and year in mono columns, download affordance on hover and focus.
- **Statistics** — each chart is one series, so each chart is one colour; cycling the discipline
  hues down six regions made the colour carry no information. Tiles follow the timing board, and
  years stop rendering with a thousands separator ("2,023").
- **Join / training / rules / news / contact / 404 / 500** — inherit the tokens, the quieter
  eyebrow, the lane rule and the new heading scale.
- **Header** — the official mark replaces the invented triangle; nav is sentence case in both
  languages; the 390 px overflow that pushed the burger 10 px off-screen is fixed.
- **Footer** — the rainbow bar becomes three discrete swim/bike/run segments, which is the code
  rather than a gradient.

## 6b. Defects found and fixed while redesigning

Three bugs that pre-date this pass and would have failed a QA round anyway:

1. **The statistics charts rendered no data.** `.bar-fill` is a `<span>` inside a grid item, so it
   was never blockified; `width` and `height` were dropped and every bar showed as an empty track.
   Fixed with `display:block` on the fill.
2. **The mobile menu bled the page through** (the P2 carried over from the Week 1 QA report). The
   drawer is a descendant of the header's `backdrop-filter` layer, where a `rgba(...,.99)`
   background composites over the blurred backdrop instead of covering it. Fixed by making
   `--nav-bg` fully opaque.
3. **The header overflowed 390 px viewports by 10 px**, pushing the burger off-screen. Fixed by
   tightening the header below 560 px and dropping the brand sub-line.

Not fixed, deliberately: `.bar-fill` animates `width` rather than a transform. It is one short,
one-shot transition on ten small elements, the element it animates is the datum itself, and
switching to `scaleX` would need `transform-origin` juggling in RTL for no measurable gain.
